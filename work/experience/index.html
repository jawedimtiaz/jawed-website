How I Approach Support Work

My approach starts with the user’s task and the impact of the issue, then narrows the scope before making changes. I aim to use the least disruptive supported checks, confirm the result against the original workflow, and leave clear documentation for the next technician.

  • Understand impact: clarify what the user needs to do, what failed and whether the issue affects one user, one device or a wider service.
  • Gather evidence: record the exact symptom, relevant context and results of checks rather than treating an error message as a confirmed root cause.
  • Act within scope: follow approved procedures and escalate when permissions, security-sensitive changes or specialist investigation are needed.
  • Validate and hand off: retest the original task, document what changed and what remains unresolved, and provide a useful next step.

Public examples of these practices are available in the selected work evidence and the technical notes. They are sanitized examples and do not disclose employer-specific systems, internal policies or user data.
