Selected Work Evidence

These are sanitized examples of the kind of practical problem solving I use in IT support. They intentionally avoid employer-specific systems, internal policies, user data and confidential operational details.

Troubleshooting

Turning First Response Into a Repeatable Workflow

Context: IT issues often arrive with incomplete symptoms and limited initial evidence.

Approach: Start with the user impact, scope the issue, gather evidence, isolate likely causes, test the smallest appropriate change and validate the result.

Outcome: A consistent first-response process makes investigation easier to follow and creates a clearer handoff when escalation is needed.

Related: Troubleshooting Workflow

Device Lifecycle

Making Device Handoffs Easier to Support

Context: Device provisioning is not finished when the device powers on; readiness and handoff details matter too.

Approach: Use a repeatable sequence covering preparation, configuration checks, validation, user handoff and support documentation.

Outcome: A structured handoff reduces ambiguity about what was completed and what the next technician or user should know.

Related: Provisioning & Handoff Checklist

Documentation

Turning Investigation Notes Into Useful Ticket History

Context: Technical work becomes harder to continue when ticket notes capture actions without enough context or validation.

Approach: Record the symptom, relevant evidence, actions taken, result and next step in a concise structure.

Outcome: Better notes make the ticket easier for another technician to understand without repeating the same investigation.

Related: Ticket Documentation Note · Note Formatter

How I Approach Practical IT Work

1. Understand the impact

Clarify what is affected, who is affected and what the user needs to accomplish.

2. Isolate the problem

Separate user, device and service factors before changing anything unnecessarily.

3. Validate and document

Confirm the result, capture useful evidence and leave a clear next step for whoever continues the work.
