AI Prompting for Everyday Work: A Simple Starting Pattern

A reusable structure for asking AI to help with writing, summarizing, research and repetitive knowledge tasks.

Published: 2026-09-28 · Updated: 2026-09-28 · 5 min read

The basic pattern

A useful prompt can start with four parts: goal, context, output and constraints.

Goal: [what I need]\nContext: [information the AI needs]\nOutput: [format, length or structure]\nConstraints: [audience, tone, limits or what to avoid]

Add an example when the output matters

If you need a specific structure or style, provide a short example. A concrete example can be more useful than many abstract instructions.

Ask for uncertainty

For research or analysis tasks, ask the AI to distinguish known information, assumptions and items that need verification. This makes review easier.

Iterate instead of writing one giant prompt

  1. Ask for a first draft or structure.
  2. Review what is missing or incorrect.
  3. Provide corrections or additional context.
  4. Ask for a revised result.

Five practical uses

  • Turn rough notes into a structured summary.
  • Create a first draft that you review and edit.
  • Extract action items from a long block of text.
  • Generate questions for research.
  • Document a repeated manual process.

What not to outsource

Do not treat generated output as automatically correct. Review important facts, calculations, confidential-information handling and consequential decisions.
