This workspace is educational and illustrative. It does not connect to accounts, recommend financial products, predict returns or provide individualized financial, investment or tax advice. Verify current rules and consider qualified professional advice for consequential decisions.

Planning review

Use this page as a structured review of the numbers you entered. It deliberately does not convert the results into a buy/sell recommendation or a personalized financial plan.

What to review

  • Is monthly spending based on a representative period?
  • Does the emergency-reserve target reflect your own circumstances?
  • Are goal costs and inflation assumptions clearly identified?
  • Are return assumptions treated as scenarios rather than promises?
  • Is retirement spending expressed in today's terms and then inflated?
  • Have you considered taxes, fees, insurance, pensions and other relevant items outside this simplified workspace?
