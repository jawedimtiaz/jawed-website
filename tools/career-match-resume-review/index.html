Career Match & Resume Review

Compare a resume with a job description in your browser and identify skills, keywords and evidence areas worth reviewing.

Browser-only. Your text is processed on this page and is not sent to an AI service or server by this tool. Avoid confidential employer information and other sensitive data.

Review

Add both texts to generate a local comparison.

How to use the result

  1. Check matching skills against what you can actually demonstrate.
  2. Review missing terms and add them only when they truthfully describe your experience.
  3. Strengthen weak resume bullets with specific actions and measurable results you can verify.
  4. Use the evidence prompts to prepare examples for interviews.

Important: This comparison is a text-overlap aid. It is not a prediction of hiring outcomes, an ATS guarantee, or a recommendation to apply, accept or reject a role.

Boundary

This tool does not contact employers, submit applications, make hiring predictions, verify credentials or determine whether you are qualified for a role.
