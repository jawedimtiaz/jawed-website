Ticket → Knowledge Base Draft

Turn verified Service Desk evidence into a reviewable knowledge-base draft without copying sensitive ticket details into a reusable article.

Browser-only. Your text stays in this page and is not sent to an AI service or server by this tool. Use sanitized examples. Do not enter passwords, authentication codes, private keys, private employee information, confidential company data, internal-only URLs or restricted security information.

Knowledge-base draft

Your draft will appear here.

Review before publishing

  • Keep only facts and procedures that were actually verified.
  • Remove ticket-specific names, identifiers and confidential details.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Confirm the validation step reproduces the expected result.
  • Check the escalation boundary and review trigger.
  • Follow the organization's documentation, security and data-handling requirements.

Boundary

This tool formats evidence; it does not decide whether a procedure is safe, approved or suitable for your organization. It does not access tickets or corporate systems and does not publish the result anywhere.
