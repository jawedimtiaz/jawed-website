Finance-tool guardrails

  • Browser-only by default; no account, broker or transaction connection required.
  • Inputs, formulas, assumptions and limitations remain visible to the user.
  • Outputs are illustrative calculations, not individualized financial, investment or tax advice.
  • Tax and other time-sensitive tools will identify the relevant country, tax year and source date before publication.
  • No stock, fund, insurer, broker or product recommendations are built into the tools.
  • Each tool should have a related educational Note explaining the underlying concept.
  • Personal finance utilities remain focused on snapshots and calculations; they do not connect to accounts or recommend financial products.

Build sequence: 14R established the growth model; 14S connects accumulation with a retirement-corpus framework. Expand only where the underlying content and use case justify another tool.
