Inflation & Goal Planning Calculator

Explore how the future cost of a goal can change when today's price is adjusted for an assumed inflation rate.

Methodology

The future goal cost is calculated as:

Future goal cost = Current goal cost × (1 + inflation rate)years

The inflation increase is the estimated future cost minus today's cost. The uncovered amount is the estimated future cost minus the amount currently set aside, floored at zero for display.

This tool does not calculate an investment return, contribution schedule, taxes, fees or product choice. It isolates the purchasing-power effect of inflation so that the assumption remains visible.

What this result does—and does not—mean

  • This is a mathematical illustration, not an inflation forecast or a recommendation.
  • Actual inflation can differ from the assumed rate and can vary significantly by category.
  • A goal may have a different inflation pattern from a broad consumer-inflation measure.
  • Current savings are shown at their stated amount; the tool does not assume those savings earn a return.
  • The calculator does not recommend a savings amount, investment, product or asset allocation.
  • For time-sensitive tax or financial rules, check current authoritative sources separately.
