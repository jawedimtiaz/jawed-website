Resume Bullet Strength Checker

Check whether your resume bullets clearly show action, evidence and impact. Paste one bullet per line.

Browser-only. Your resume text stays in this page. Nothing is uploaded or sent to an AI service.

This checker is a writing diagnostic, not an official ATS score. A strong result still depends on truthful, role-relevant evidence.

Results

Paste your bullets and run the checker.

What it checks

  • Action language: flags common weak openings such as “responsible for” or “worked on”.
  • Evidence: checks for numbers, percentages, currency, counts or time-based measures.
  • Length: flags very short or overly long bullets that may need clarification or trimming.
  • Filler: flags vague phrases that do not demonstrate what you actually did.
