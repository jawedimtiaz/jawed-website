## How to write a cover letter for IT jobs

A good IT cover letter is short, specific and honest. It should connect two or three things you have actually done to what the job asks for, in about 150 to 250 words.

- **Paragraph 1:** the role, the company and your strongest matching skills.
- **Paragraph 2:** one real achievement with scale or a number you can verify, such as users supported, sites covered or hours saved.
- **Paragraph 3:** one specific reason you want this employer, taken from their website or posting.
- **Close:** a polite thank-you, with no promises or pressure.

This tool only uses what you type. Anything you leave blank appears as a bracketed "Not Provided" placeholder so you never send invented claims. Always edit the letter in your own voice before sending.

## FAQ

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Saved letters stay in your own browser storage.

Do I need a cover letter for IT roles?

Many employers do not require one, but a short, tailored letter helps when you are changing fields, have gaps, or the posting asks for it.

Can the tool guarantee a reply?

No. It helps you write clearly, but hiring decisions depend on many factors outside any tool.
