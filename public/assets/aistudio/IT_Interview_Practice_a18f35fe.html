How to prepare for an IT interview

IT interviews usually mix three things: core technical knowledge, practical troubleshooting, and behavioural questions about how you work. This tool gives you a question, a space to answer out loud or in writing, and a checklist of the points a strong answer usually covers, so you can see which ideas you missed.

  • Answer troubleshooting questions in order: scope the problem, check the basics, narrow down, fix, verify, document.
  • Use the STAR method for behavioural questions: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Spend most of your time on what you personally did.
  • Be honest. Say what you have actually done. It is fine to say "I have not used that, but here is how I would approach it".
  • Practise out loud with the timer. Two minutes is a good target for most answers.

The key points shown are common talking points, not scripts or guaranteed answers. Interviewers differ, so adapt to the job description and your own experience.

FAQ

Is my answer stored or sent anywhere?

No. The text box is never saved or uploaded. Only your self-rated coverage scores are kept in your browser.

Will practising here guarantee I pass?

No. It helps you rehearse and spot gaps, but results depend on the interview, the role and many other factors.

Can I add my own questions?

Not in this version. Use the job description to write your own and practise them the same way.
