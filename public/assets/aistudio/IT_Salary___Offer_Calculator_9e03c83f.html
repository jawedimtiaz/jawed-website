Work out your hike, compare two job offers realistically, and estimate a notice-period buyout. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use these calculators

Hike: enter your current pay and the new offer to see the increase in money and percent, or enter a hike percentage to see what it means in rupees. Compare offers: a higher headline CTC is not always better. Variable pay is usually not guaranteed, so this tool lets you enter the payout you realistically expect (for example 80% of target) and adds joining bonus and other annual benefits. Notice buyout: a quick estimate of the cost of leaving before your notice period ends.

  • Tax and provident fund are not looked up for you. Tax rules and slabs change, so enter your own effective tax percentage and monthly deductions from your payslip or a tax professional.
  • Results are estimates for comparison, not tax, legal or financial advice.
  • Always confirm what is fixed, what is variable and what is a one-time payment before accepting an offer.

FAQ

Does this send my salary anywhere?

No. All calculations run in your browser and nothing is stored or uploaded.

What is a good hike when changing jobs?

It depends on role, skills, market and location. Use the calculator to see your number, then compare it with salary surveys for your role and city.

Why does the tool ask for my tax percentage?

Because the correct figure depends on your regime, deductions and year. Entering your own keeps the estimate honest instead of guessing.
