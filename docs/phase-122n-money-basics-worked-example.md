# Phase 122N — Money Basics: Practical Examples and Local Reference

Status: **Changes prepared; CI and deployment verification pending**

## Audit finding

The money-basics note defined budgeting, emergency funds and net worth but relied on brief descriptions. It did not show how to apply the concepts to a simple fictional monthly example. Its emergency-fund link was also US-based despite the page's Indian audience.

## Changes made

- Added a clearly fictional monthly cash-flow table.
- Added three- and six-month emergency-reserve arithmetic using the example's essential expenses, while explaining that the appropriate target depends on circumstances.
- Added a fictional net-worth calculation and clarified that net worth does not equal liquid cash.
- Replaced the US-oriented emergency-fund reference with Reserve Bank of India financial-education material.
- Updated the visible page revision date and refreshed/synchronized the AI knowledge entry.

## Safeguards

- Figures are explicitly illustrative, not a recommended household budget or personalized target.
- No investment product or transaction is recommended.
- Existing financial-content disclosure remains in place.
- No new route, sitemap change or AdSense activation change.

## Verification

Require site-integrity, AI regression, AdSense readiness, security, SEO, performance and preview checks. After merge, verify the main commit and successful Cloudflare Pages production deployment.

## Decision

The note now demonstrates the concepts with transparent arithmetic and a locally relevant educational source. It remains general education, not individualized financial advice.
