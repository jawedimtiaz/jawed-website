# Phase 122G — Original IT Triage Case Study

Status: **Article and governance updates prepared; CI and deployment verification pending**

## Purpose

The blog collection was small, and the existing IT notes primarily provided reusable checklists and frameworks. This phase adds one original long-form walkthrough demonstrating how those principles apply to a clearly fictional scenario, rather than duplicating the checklist format.

## New route

`/blog/triage-workplace-app-access-issue/`

The article walks through:
- defining the user task and impact;
- scoping a problem across user, device, application and service;
- recording observations before changing anything;
- selecting one safe, discriminating check;
- separating observed facts from hypotheses;
- validating the original workflow;
- preparing a clear escalation and protecting sensitive information.

The article explicitly labels the scenario as fictional and explains the limits of a generic example.

## Safety and editorial safeguards

- No real incident or employer-specific case is claimed.
- No confidential hostnames, user details, credentials or internal operational information are included.
- The walkthrough does not prescribe a universal fix or encourage bypassing security controls.
- It emphasizes approved support procedures, least-disruptive checks, validation and escalation.
