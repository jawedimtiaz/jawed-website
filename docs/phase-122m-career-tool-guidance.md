# Phase 122M — Career Tool Guidance

Status: **Changes prepared; CI and deployment verification pending**

## Audit finding

The Interview Preparation Planner and Resume Bullet Strength Checker are working browser-based utilities, but the surrounding instructions did not fully explain how to use their outputs responsibly.

## Changes made

- Added a step-by-step method for turning an interview plan into real, role-relevant evidence and practice.
- Clarified that keyword detection cannot predict an employer's actual interview format or criteria.
- Added a resume bullet revision pattern based on action, relevant scope/method and verified result.
- Added an illustrative rewrite and a warning not to invent numbers or outcomes.
- Updated and synchronized the two corresponding AI knowledge entries.

## Safeguards

- No real resume, job description or personal data was added to the repository.
- No hiring prediction or official ATS-score claim is made.
- No new routes, sitemap changes or monetization activation changes.
