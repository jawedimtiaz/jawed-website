Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is handled on jawed.co.in. The policy will be kept aligned with the site's actual functionality.

Information Collected

The website does not require visitors to create an account simply to browse the site. The contact form opens the visitor's email application to send a message to webmaster@jawed.co.in; the website itself does not store submitted messages.

Cookies and Third-party Services

The current site does not require analytics, advertising or an external form service to provide its core functionality. If those services are introduced later, this policy will be updated to describe them.

External Links

The site may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are outside the control of jawed.co.in.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the site's functionality changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact webmaster@jawed.co.in.
