Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is handled on jawed.co.in. The policy is kept aligned with the site's actual functionality.

Information Collected

The website does not require visitors to create an account simply to browse the site. The contact form opens the visitor's email application to send a message to webmaster@jawed.co.in; the website itself does not store submitted messages.

Browser Tools

Many tools on this site process the information you enter locally in your browser rather than sending it to a site server. For example, the Service Desk Note Formatter, AI Prompt Builder, IT Troubleshooting Assistant, Ticket → Knowledge Base Draft, finance calculators and Finance Planning Workspace are browser-based utilities.

The Career Match & Resume Review tool processes resume and job-description text in the browser and does not send that text to a server for analysis. If you choose Save Analysis, generated analysis, Application Tracker entries and the theme preference are stored in your browser's local storage until you delete them. PDF and DOCX reading uses third-party browser libraries loaded from cdnjs, so those file-reading features require an internet connection. Use browser tools only with information you are permitted to process and avoid confidential employer, customer or other restricted information.

Individual tool pages explain any tool-specific storage, external library or data-handling behavior. Tools do not connect to accounts, brokers, transaction systems or employer systems unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and usability. Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, interactions, approximate location, browser and device information, and uses first-party identifiers such as the _ga cookie. The site does not intentionally send personally identifiable information to Google Analytics.

For more information about Google Analytics data collection and privacy controls, see Google Analytics data collection and Google's Privacy Policy.

External Links

The site may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are outside the control of jawed.co.in.

Cookies and Third-party Services

Google Analytics uses first-party cookies and similar technologies for measurement. Resource links may take you to third-party websites, which have their own policies and practices.

Advertising and AdSense

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on this website, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising, including ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. Google's advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to personalize ads where permitted.

When advertising is enabled, this policy will identify the advertising providers actually used on the site and explain the relevant choices available to visitors. Users can manage Google personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Where applicable, users may also use the privacy or consent controls presented on the site.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, personalized advertising will only be served using a consent approach that meets Google's applicable publisher requirements. Google currently requires a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework when serving personalized ads to users in those regions.

The site is connected to the publisher's AdSense account for site verification and review. The publisher identifier used for that connection is not itself an advertising cookie or ad-serving script. Advertising cookies, advertising scripts, ad slots, and advertising partners will be introduced only when the corresponding advertising service is actually enabled.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the site's functionality changes.
