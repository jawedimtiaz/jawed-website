Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is handled on jawed.co.in. The policy is kept aligned with the site's actual functionality, Google AdSense mandatory publisher policies, and relevant legal standards.

Data Collection Practices and Information Collected

The website does not require visitors to create an account simply to browse the site. We maintain clear and transparent data collection practices across our features:

Contact Form Submissions

When you use the contact form, you enter your name, email address, topic, and message. Submitting this form opens your device's default email client addressed to webmaster@jawed.co.in. The website does not store, transmit to an external database, or sell your submitted messages or contact details to third parties or ad networks. Information sent via email is used exclusively to respond to your inquiry.

Site Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with content and to improve website performance and usability. Google Analytics collects standard visitor interaction data using first-party identifiers (such as the _ga cookie). The site does not intentionally send personally identifiable information to Google Analytics. For more information about Google Analytics data collection and privacy controls, see Google Analytics data collection and Google's Privacy Policy.

IP Addresses and Hosting Logs

Like most web applications, our hosting infrastructure and content delivery network (Cloudflare) temporarily process visitor IP addresses for network routing, security monitoring, DDoS mitigation, and geographic performance optimization. IP addresses collected in server logs are not merged with personally identifiable information or used for profiling.

Browser Tools

Many tools on this site process the information you enter locally in your browser rather than sending it to a site server. For example, the Service Desk Note Formatter, AI Prompt Builder, IT Troubleshooting Assistant, Ticket → Knowledge Base Draft, finance calculators and Finance Planning Workspace are browser-based utilities.

The Career Match & Resume Review tool processes resume and job-description text in the browser and does not send that text to a server for analysis. If you choose Save Analysis, generated analysis, Application Tracker entries and the theme preference are stored in your browser's local storage until you delete them. PDF and DOCX reading uses third-party browser libraries loaded from cdnjs, so those file-reading features require an internet connection. Use browser tools only with information you are permitted to process and avoid confidential employer, customer or other restricted information.

Individual tool pages explain any tool-specific storage, external library or data-handling behavior. Tools do not connect to accounts, brokers, transaction systems or employer systems unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

AI Assistant and Conversation Data

When you submit a question to Jawed AI, the website sends the question and recent conversation context to its server-side AI endpoint to generate a response. When the service is configured, the endpoint sends the conversation content and relevant public-site reference material to Cloudflare Workers AI for processing.

The application's diagnostic logs are designed to record request identifiers and operational metadata rather than the message text. This does not override processing by the hosting or AI service provider under its applicable terms and privacy practices. This policy does not make a claim about provider-side retention.

Do not enter passwords, authentication codes, private keys, sensitive personal information, confidential employer or customer information, or details from restricted support cases. Treat AI output as a starting point and verify consequential information against authoritative sources.

For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and Third-party Services

Google Analytics uses first-party cookies and similar technologies for measurement. Resource links may take you to third-party websites, which have their own policies and practices.

Advertising and AdSense

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on this website, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising, including ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. Google's advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to personalize ads where permitted.

Third-Party Vendors and Ad Networks

Third-party vendors and ad networks, specifically Google AdSense, serve advertisements on this website. These vendors may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information as a result of ad serving on this site.

Mandatory Cookie Disclosure and the DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie and other advertising identifiers, to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. When users view or click an ad, a cookie may be dropped on that end user's browser. Google's use of the DoubleClick DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

User Opt-Out Instructions

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com/ or https://www.google.com/settings/ads. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice page at http://www.aboutads.info/choices/.

Regional Consent Requirements

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, personalized advertising will only be served using a consent approach that meets Google's applicable publisher requirements. Google currently requires a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework when serving personalized ads to users in those regions.

Site Verification and Ad Serving

The site is connected to the publisher's AdSense account for site verification and review. The publisher identifier used for that connection is not itself an advertising cookie or ad-serving script. Advertising cookies, advertising scripts, ad slots, and advertising partners will be introduced only when the corresponding advertising service is actually enabled.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the site's functionality changes.
