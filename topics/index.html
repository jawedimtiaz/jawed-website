How content is organized

Each piece of content has a clear topic, purpose and format. This keeps the site useful as it grows instead of turning it into a collection of unrelated posts.

Guide

Explains a practical concept or process step by step.

Checklist

Turns a repeatable task into a concise sequence of actions.

Analysis

Examines a topic, comparison, trend or decision framework using clearly stated assumptions and sources where appropriate.

Experience

Documents something I personally built, tested, learned or changed, clearly separated from general advice.

Tool

Provides an interactive utility that helps a visitor apply a concept or complete a task.

Publishing principles

  • Useful first: publish when there is a real problem, question or learning worth documenting.
  • Original perspective: add practical experience, methodology, examples or analysis rather than rewriting generic information.
  • Clear context: distinguish personal experience from general educational information.
  • Finance care: investing, tax, retirement and insurance content will use dates, assumptions and relevant sources, and will not be presented as individualized financial advice.
  • AI care: describe practical uses and limitations rather than publishing generic AI news for volume.
  • Maintainability: review time-sensitive information when it can materially change.
  • No empty categories: a topic becomes active only when there is worthwhile content to support it.

Publishing sequence

  1. Strengthen the foundation: add several high-quality AI and technology pieces that connect naturally to existing Notes and Work evidence.
  2. Introduce finance carefully: start with evergreen educational concepts before adding time-sensitive market or tax material.
  3. Turn useful ideas into tools: build calculators or small utilities after the underlying content proves useful.
  4. Connect the library: every new article should link to its Topic, related Notes, relevant Tools and other supporting content where appropriate.
  5. Review before publishing: check accuracy, originality, date sensitivity, disclosure and whether the piece adds something genuinely useful.

Evergreen vs. timely

Evergreen

Concepts, workflows, checklists, fundamentals and practical frameworks. These form the durable search and reference layer of the site.

Timely

Market conditions, product changes, tax-year rules, AI releases and other changing information. These should be explicitly dated and reviewed or updated when necessary.

Content metadata & editorial standards

A consistent publishing standard for future Notes, Blog posts, Analysis and other substantial content.

Every substantial article

  • Clear title and concise description
  • Author: Jawed Imtiaz
  • Published date
  • Last updated date when changed materially
  • Topic and subtopic
  • Content type
  • Audience intent

Useful context

  • Reading time when it helps set expectations
  • Related Notes, Topics and Tools
  • Sources or references when claims depend on external information
  • Clear distinction between general education and personal experience

Finance & investing

  • State the relevant country, tax year or market period
  • Show important assumptions and methodology
  • Date time-sensitive facts
  • Identify sources where appropriate
  • Keep educational information separate from individualized advice

AI & changing technology

  • Identify the product, model or version when relevant
  • Separate observed behavior from general claims
  • Include limitations or important caveats
  • Review material that can become outdated

Editorial quality gate

  1. Purpose: What question or practical problem does this content answer?
  2. Originality: What useful experience, explanation, framework or analysis does it add?
  3. Accuracy: Are important factual claims supported and appropriately dated?
  4. Clarity: Can the intended reader understand what to do or learn?
  5. Context: Are assumptions, limitations, disclosures and personal-experience boundaries clear?
  6. Maintenance: Does the page need an update trigger because the subject changes over time?
  7. Discovery: Does it link naturally to the relevant Topic and related content?

How the site is organized

Topics is the subject-level discovery layer. Notes holds shorter practical guides, Blog holds longer-form experiences and analysis, and Tools contains interactive utilities. Work remains the professional profile and evidence layer.
