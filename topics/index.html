How content is organized

Each piece of content has a clear topic, purpose and format. This keeps the site useful as it grows instead of turning it into a collection of unrelated posts.

Guide

Explains a practical concept or process step by step.

Checklist

Turns a repeatable task into a concise sequence of actions.

Analysis

Examines a topic, comparison, trend or decision framework using clearly stated assumptions and sources where appropriate.

Experience

Documents something I personally built, tested, learned or changed, clearly separated from general advice.

Tool

Provides an interactive utility that helps a visitor apply a concept or complete a task.

Publishing principles

  • Useful first: publish when there is a real problem, question or learning worth documenting.
  • Original perspective: add practical experience, methodology, examples or analysis rather than rewriting generic information.
  • Clear context: distinguish personal experience from general educational information.
  • Finance care: investing, tax, retirement and insurance content will use dates, assumptions and relevant sources, and will not be presented as individualized financial advice.
  • AI care: describe practical uses and limitations rather than publishing generic AI news for volume.
  • Maintainability: review time-sensitive information when it can materially change.
  • No empty categories: a topic becomes active only when there is worthwhile content to support it.

How the site is organized

Topics is the subject-level discovery layer. Notes holds shorter practical guides, Blog holds longer-form experiences and analysis, and Tools contains interactive utilities. Work remains the professional profile and evidence layer.
