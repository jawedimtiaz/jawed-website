Rebuilding Jawed.co.in

How I moved an old personal website to a simpler, modern foundation built around the work I do today and the topics I want to document next.

September 2026

Why rebuild?

Jawed.co.in started as a personal website during the early part of my IT career. It grew around technical certification study material, useful references and a collection of other content. Over the years, much of that technology and many of those pages became outdated.

Instead of trying to modernize every old page, I chose to audit the site, recover useful personal history and rebuild the public structure from scratch.

What changed?

  • The old fixed-layout pages and legacy runtime dependencies were removed.
  • Obsolete certification and technology collections were retired rather than carried forward.
  • The new site uses a small, mobile-first HTML, CSS and JavaScript foundation.
  • Work, Notes, Blog, Tools and Resources now provide the main structure for future content.
  • GitHub is the source of truth and Cloudflare Pages handles deployment.

What I kept

The rebuild keeps the useful part of the site's history: who I am, my career journey, why the site was started and the professional direction I am working in today.

What comes next

The new foundation is intentionally small. I plan to add technical notes, practical tools, useful resources and longer-form posts as there is material worth publishing. The goal is to grow the site through useful content rather than simply recreating the size of the old site.
